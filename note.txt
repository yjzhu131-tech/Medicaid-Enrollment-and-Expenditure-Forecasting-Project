Current analysis scope (updated October 14, 2026)

All existing enrollment and expenditure forecasts now use Medicaid + CHIP combined.
Enrollment retains Total Medicaid Enrollment, Total CHIP Enrollment, and Total Medicaid and CHIP Enrollment. Only updated (U) records from June 2017 onward are retained.
Expenditure retains original Medicaid MAP and adds CHIP components and the combined total in data/clean/expenditure_clean_medicaid_chip_total.csv, FY1997–FY2024.
CHIP = MCHIP C-Total Net + MCHIP 20% T-Total Net Expenditures (where reported) + separate CHIP Total. The separate CHIP total includes reported administration; Medicaid ADM remains excluded, consistent with the original MAP series. Therefore the combined expenditure is not a pure benefits-only measure.
National results use the same 50 states plus DC as the enrollment data, excluding expenditure-only territories.
Per-enrollee spending divides fiscal-year expenditure by mean monthly combined enrollment from the same federal fiscal year (October–September). FY2017 has only June–September (4 months) and is marked as partial coverage.
Existing models, settings, forecast horizons, and output filenames are retained. Forecast intervals remain the original model's approximate intervals.

Rebuild all outputs and the advisor Word document:
python3 -m pip install -r requirements.txt
python3 refresh_results.py
